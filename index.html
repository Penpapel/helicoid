DACTYL

A synthesizer you play with your fingers. Every gesture shapes the sound.

  • Drag

    Left and right sets the pitch. Up and down opens the filter.

  • Scrub

    Move fast to push the filter into resonance.

  • Hold

    Keep still for half a second to latch a drone. Tap the ring to let it go.

  • Flick

    Lift your finger while moving to throw the sound into the echo.

  • Two fingers

    Spread to widen the unison. Twist to morph the waveform from sine to square.

  • Layers

    Each circle at the top is a layer with its own sound. Tap one to play it, tap it again to turn it off. Hold to switch it without changing layers. Plus adds a layer.

  • Loop

    Press Rec, play, press Stop. Your gestures repeat on that layer. Later layers record in time with the first loop.

  • Double tap

    Releases every latched drone at once.

  • Auto-compose

    Writes a new piece on six layers: tempo, key, chords, drums, melody and air. The parts come in and out and the piece keeps changing. Play over it, or record your own loops on top.

  • Click

    Switch on the click for a steady beat in your ears. Set the tempo in Patch. Rec then counts you in and trims your first loop to whole bars, and the click follows the loop after that.

  • Export

    Open Patch and scroll to Export. Bounce a loop as a seamless WAV, record a take of whatever you play, or save the loops as MIDI.

Auto-compose replaces your current layers and loops. Press it again for a new piece.
